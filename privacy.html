Gorilla Warden — Privacy Policy

VELRAN GAMEWORKS · Last updated: 2 October 2026

This policy explains what data the VR game Gorilla Warden (Meta Quest) collects, why, who it is shared with, and your rights. Gorilla Warden is developed by VELRAN GAMEWORKS ("we", "us"), based in France. Contact: velrangameworks@proton.me.

1. Data we collect

We do not collect your real name, email, location, payment details or profile photo. We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it.

2. Who we share data with

  • Meta Platforms — the Meta Horizon Store and platform features (accounts, friends, invitations, block and report flows).
  • Microsoft Corporation (PlayFab) — game backend: player account (Meta user ID, display name), friends, block list and report records. Servers in the United States.
  • Normal VR Inc. (Normcore) — real-time multiplayer and voice servers. Voice and movement data pass through their servers during a session and are not stored. Servers in the United States and other regions.

These providers process data only on our behalf. Some data is therefore processed outside the European Union, under the providers' standard safeguards.

3. Other players

Other players in your room see your in-game name, avatar and colour, and hear your voice when voice chat is on. You can mute, block or report any player from the lobby computer, and report through the Meta button (Meta's official report flow).

4. Children

Gorilla Warden is available to a mixed audience. For accounts that Meta identifies as children, voice chat is off by default and can be switched on from the game's settings. We collect no more data from children than needed to run the game.

5. How long we keep data

Account data (ID, name, friends, blocks) is kept while your account is active and deleted on request. Report records are kept up to 12 months. Voice and movement data are never stored.

6. Requests from public authorities

We review the legality of any request from a public authority, challenge requests we consider unlawful, disclose only the minimum necessary, and keep a record of each request and our response.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by emailing velrangameworks@proton.me (include your in-game name). We answer within 30 days. In the EU you can also complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
